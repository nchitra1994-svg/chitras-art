About

I’m a professional graphic designer and artist who loves to explore the world of art. My paintings move freely between styles and subjects, from mural-inspired work and still life to watercolour, mixed media and bold, colourful canvases. Every piece begins with a story, and I hope each one finds a home where it can tell it.
